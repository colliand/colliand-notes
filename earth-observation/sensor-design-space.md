Started at SatSummit 2026 in St. Louis. Most of the talks assumed you already knew why one satellite carries
a C-band radar and another a 22-band optical camera. I want a map of that design space.

## Questions

- What do the radar bands (X, C, L, and others) trade off: wavelength, resolution, penetration through cloud,
  canopy and soil, and revisit?
- What separates panchromatic, multispectral and hyperspectral optical sensors? Why does band count matter?
- Active versus passive sensing: what does radar see that optical can't, and the reverse?
- Where do the big constellations sit on this map? Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2, Landsat, NISAR, Planet
  (about 700 optical satellites), EarthDaily (22 bands, 10 satellites).
- Non-Earth imaging: satellites that image other satellites. What is that market, and which sensors does it use?
